The read-back loader

Loads finance entries one at a time and refuses to call an entry done until the system of record agrees with it.

  1. 1Dry run on the whole file
  2. 2Write one entry
  3. 3Read it back from the system
  4. 4Match it field by field, or flag it

06 · Questions

Questions people ask

What does "read back" mean?

After a figure is written, it is fetched again from the system of record and matched field by field. A mismatch is flagged and never counted as done.

What is Read Back?

My free weekly newsletter on LinkedIn. Each issue reads one Nifty 50 company's latest results line by line, with its sources. It is not investment advice.

Who is this site for?

Finance, operations and MBA readers who want to read a P&L, and teams that need a number they can stand behind.

What do you do for companies?

I find where a number goes wrong, build the read-back that keeps it right, and teach your team to ask the number questions with AI. It is mentoring and advisory.
