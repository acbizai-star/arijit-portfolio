02 · Selected work

Three problems, one pattern

Different industries, same first move: find out what the numbers are actually saying before touching the process.

01Fintech / BNPL
Simpl

A collections engine losing money on its own recoveries

Situation200+ person collections org, portfolio scope stuck at DPD 90, dormant accounts untouched. ActionRe-engineered the recovery workflow, extended scope to DPD 180+, put speech analytics into the call flow, rebuilt the dialer stack for real-time tracking.
Result — GMV up 1.5×, delinquency down ~50%, cost per recovery 22→18 bps
02Quick commerce
Swiggy

A city with no delivery operation, six months to build one

SituationPatna had zero infrastructure — no fleet, no ops playbook, no local team. ActionBuilt the fleet, dispatch model and city ops team from scratch; later optimised batching and cost-per-delivery across Kolkata's wider network.
Result — 7,000+ orders/day within 6 months; CPD down ~20% network-wide
03Compliance
Amazon
